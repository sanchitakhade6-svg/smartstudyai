The problem

Studying hard isn't the same as studying well.

Most students revise everything equally — even the parts they already know — because there's no easy way to see what's actually weak.

01

Passive, inefficient study habits lead to poor retention, even after hours of reading.

02

One-size-fits-all study tools ignore each student's pace and learning style.

03

Without instant feedback, self-assessment before an exam is little more than guesswork.

04

Exam prep is time-consuming, and finding your own weak areas is genuinely hard to do alone.
